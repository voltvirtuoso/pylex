# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community. Participants should communicate professionally, assume good faith, focus on the technical issue, and respect different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, publishing private information, and deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Report concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or the maintainer’s GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
